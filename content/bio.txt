Rayhan Khimji (he/him) is a stage and screen actor who trained in the BFA Acting program at Point Park University.

Hey what's up y’all! My name is Rayhan Alkarim Sherali Abdul-Rasul Kassamali Khimji, or at least it was supposed to be. My father removed 4 of his 7 names our family had held for generations when he moved to the United States so it would fit on an American passport.

As a first-generation Indian-American artist, I strive to be an actor that illuminates experiences from the communities I come from, and am always trying to “find a way home” through my work. My hope is to be a role model to those of similar backgrounds and histories, and inspire the artists of the next generation through representation, storytelling, and empathy. I am drawn to stories of moral philosophy, the “why” behind who we are as people.
